# BNP Paribas SA Financial Analysis (FY2022–FY2024)

A professional three-year financial analysis of BNP Paribas SA using publicly available annual reports, financial statements, Pillar 3 disclosures, and investor reporting.

This project demonstrates the analytical, technical, and reporting skills expected of a Financial Analyst, Banking Analyst, Risk Analyst, or Big 4 Financial Services Consultant operating in the Luxembourg and wider European financial-services sector.

---

## Project Objective

The purpose of this project is to evaluate BNP Paribas SA's financial performance, capital strength, liquidity position, credit quality, and operating efficiency over FY2022–FY2024.

The analysis is designed to answer the following business question:

> How did BNP Paribas perform through a changing European interest-rate cycle, and what are the key implications for profitability, solvency, liquidity, and credit quality?

## Important Limitations

### IFRS 17 / IFRS 9 Transition

BNP Paribas adopted IFRS 17 and related insurance IFRS 9 changes from 1 January 2023.

FY2022 figures in this analysis are intentionally retained on the originally reported basis to preserve three-statement reconciliation within the model.

An accompanying restatement schedule documents the known reported-versus-restated differences identified during the review process.

### Additional Limitations

- Annual data only
- No forecasting model
- No valuation model
- No stress testing
- No investment recommendation
- No claim of regulatory validation or supervisory approval
